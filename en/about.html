Our story

Experience you feel on every trip

Alfa Tours was born with a simple idea: that arriving should be as good as the destination. With more than two decades of experience, we've become a benchmark in tourist transport, specialists in school trips and partners to companies and event organizers.

Every year we move thousands of people with the same promise: safety first of all, comfort every mile and personalized attention before, during and after the trip.
