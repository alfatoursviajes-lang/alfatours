Privacy notice

How we take care of the information you share when you request a quote or book a trip.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Data controller

Silvio Segura Rojo, doing business as Transportes Turísticos Alfa Tours, based in Mazatlán, Sinaloa, Mexico, is responsible for the personal data you provide when you request a quote or book a transportation service.

What data we collect

When you contact us via WhatsApp, by phone or through this website, we may collect:

  • Your name and, where applicable, the organization you represent.
  • Your phone number and, if you share it, your email address.
  • Trip details: origin, destination, dates and times, number of passengers, stops and itinerary.
  • Billing and contract details (tax ID, representative, pick-up location), only if you book the service.

We do not request sensitive personal data.

How we use it

Required purposes:

  1. To answer your request and prepare your quote.
  2. To prepare and follow up on the contract and the transportation service.
  3. To invoice and collect payment for the service.
  4. To remind you of the dates and details of the booked service.

Optional purpose (you can opt out without affecting the service):

  1. To let you know about promotions or availability. To opt out, write to viajes@alfatours.mx or via WhatsApp at 669 116 0439.

How to limit the use or disclosure of your data

At any time you can ask us to stop using your data for the optional purpose (promotions) or to stop contacting you, by writing to viajes@alfatours.mx or via WhatsApp at 669 116 0439. We add your number to our internal list so you no longer receive promotions.

How we use technology

We use Meta's WhatsApp Business platform to receive and answer messages, and our own software assistant to organize quote requests. This assistant:

  • Processes messages on the company's own servers located in Mexico.
  • Does not make decisions on its own: every price and every commercial reply is reviewed and approved by a person at Alfa Tours.
  • Only handles customer conversations. Non-business messages are not stored and never leave our servers.
  • Only in exceptional cases, anonymized parts of a business request (no name or phone number) may be processed by a cloud AI provider to interpret it.

Who we share your data with

We never sell or rent your data. We only share it with:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp), as the messaging service provider.
  • Providers that help us operate (website hosting, electronic invoicing), only for the purposes in this notice.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep quote and contract data for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet tax and legal obligations (usually 5 years). After that it is deleted or anonymized.

Your rights and how to delete your data

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, and withdraw your consent. Write to viajes@alfatours.mx or via WhatsApp at 669 116 0439 with your name, the phone number you contacted us from and your request.

We will answer within 20 business days of receiving your request and, if applicable, carry it out within the following 15 business days. To give you access to your data we will ask you to verify your identity or that of your legal representative. If you ask for deletion, we remove your data from our assistant and our records, except what the law requires us to keep.

If you disagree with our answer, you may file a data protection request with Mexico's Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno within 15 business days of receiving it.

Changes to this notice

Any change will be published on this same page with its update date. If a change affects the purposes or how we share your data, we will also let you know through the channel you used to contact us (WhatsApp or email).

Terms of use of our channels

Our WhatsApp and website channels are for requesting information, quotes and services. Quotes are informational until a contract is signed. Prices are always confirmed by a person at Alfa Tours.
